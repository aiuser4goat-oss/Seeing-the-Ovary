Completed paired objective experiment (rejected):
A matched frozen-feature comparison between record-equivalent weighted soft cross-entropy and candidate-set marginal loss. Candidate sets come from identical FIT inputs only. Every source record is represented. Soft-target aggregation is mathematically equivalent to the original record-weighted cross-entropy for identical predictions. Unlike soft CE, marginal loss discards within-set target proportions while retaining candidate membership and total group mass. The marginal objective changes the training assumption: a supplied candidate is treated as an alternative, not a newly verified clinical label. The method is inspired by partial-label research, not an implementation of PRODEN or PiCO. A group containing all classes contributes no marginal supervision. Its benefit must be measured against the matched control; no improvement is assumed.

Implementation: accuracy_development/group_targets.py; tools/compare_group_objectives.py. Five tests verify record-objective equivalence, analytic gradients using finite differences, singleton reduction, fitting-only guard and invalid-weight rejection. Source/evaluation labels remain unchanged.
