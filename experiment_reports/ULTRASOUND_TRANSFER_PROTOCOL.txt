ANATOMY AUDIT
Reverified cached follicle instance truth for all 100 anatomy-validation slices against original USOVA r1 files. This is 100 correlated slices from only two volumes (90 contain annotated ovary, 87 contain annotated follicles), not 100 independent cases. Cached probabilities are float16. These observations already selected postprocessing earlier, so all new crop checks are descriptive development results, not independent confirmation.

The original crop had mean ovarian annotation coverage 96.7703%, with minimum 2.0169%. Five positive slices retained less than 90% of the annotated ovary. Expanded context plus a fallback based on the ORIGINAL crop proposal raised mean coverage to 99.9798%, minimum 98.1785%, and follicle annotation coverage to 100% on this sample. The mean selected image area increased from 57.4379% to 79.7274%; fallbacks increased from 11 to 16 of 100. Larger crops/full-image fallback naturally improve coverage, and these statistics do not establish improved segmentation, classification or generalization.

Experimental guard: use original 10%-margin crop for the size check. If predicted mask fraction is <1% or that crop occupies <15% of padded image area, retain the full image and mark ALL morphology missing. Otherwise expand context to a 25% margin. Expanding context cannot bypass the original size check. These thresholds are exploratory choices informed by the validation audit, not verified anatomical-quality thresholds. No ovary/follicle diagnosis is inferred by the guard.

Re-ran the frozen segmenter on a deterministic stratified sample of 16 fitting/selection classification images. No independent anatomy annotations exist for that sample. Overlays are predictions, not verified anatomy. The sample exposed regions requiring caution; it does not permit numerical anatomical-accuracy claims on the classification distribution. No classification targets were edited. The guard returned the full image for 2/16 sampled images. The primary pipeline and frozen feature caches are untouched; guard code is isolated under accuracy_development.

PARALLEL WEIGHTING STUDY
One fixed previously selected frozen-feature logistic readout (pool+head, C=.1) compared inverse-square-root duplicate weights with inverse-count weights, recalculating the existing capped class weights on FIT only. Every FIT record kept a positive weight. Control reproduced 87.0271% selection BA; treatment reached 86.6818%. Macro F1 increased .79451 to .80736, but BA is the target. Reject the treatment. This changed the combined duplicate/class weighting rule, not source labels or training roles, and did not modify the main GPU recipe. See duplicate_weighting_study/declaration.json and results.json.
